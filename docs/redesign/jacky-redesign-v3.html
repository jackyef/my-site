About

README 📖

A user guide
for working with me.

Hey! 👋 This document speaks about my values and how they shape how I work. A user manual for me — hopefully it helps us understand each other better.

Living document · Updated 2024 · Send feedback

01
Honesty & Transparency
These are the foundation of trust.
I try to be open about things that affect our work. Giving honest feedback — with good intentions — matters a lot to me.
02
Longing for Clarity
I believe we work better when we're in sync. I'll always ask when I have doubts — please do the same. It's never a bother.
03
Integrity
It's very hard for me to work with people I don't trust.
Trust is built by following through, admitting mistakes, and not sacrificing others for selfish reasons. Consistently, over time.
04
Work is part of life
Most people work to live, not live to work. If life gets in the way, I try to be understanding and accommodating. People fulfilled outside of work are genuinely better to work with.
05
Async-first
Async comms require effort — but it's worth it.
It's more inclusive and lets people work at their own pace. I default to async, though sync is sometimes the right call.
06
Assuming Good Intent
I don't like making bad assumptions. If I have doubts, I'll ask directly. And yes — I like to meme, in good faith 😄.
07
Introversion & Directness
Predominantly introverted. I don't usually speak unless asked, but I'm not afraid to say no — I'd rather set clear expectations than leave ambiguity.
08
Getting Better
Strive to be better than your past self.
Not necessarily every day — but the trend should move upward. I believe we work better when we want to get better.
09
Impact through Helping
When in doubt, I default to helping — improving processes, writing docs, sharing knowledge, or just not being a blocker.
10
Appreciating Affirmation
I often second-guess myself. Affirmation from people I work with means a lot — it helps me know where I'm doing well and where to improve. I'll do the same for you.

If we're working together…

A few more things

More role-specific. May change over time.

I believe you're good at what you do
I respect the hiring process. We're collectively equipped to solve hard problems.
I care about the long-term
I want our team to be a place we're happy to work. This means I can be cautious or nitpicky about things that matter over time.
English isn't my first language
If I use a word wrong, please ask rather than assuming ill intent. I'm always learning.
My hours are dynamic
No expectation to reply immediately to late messages. I keep things balanced.
TL;DR
Be honest, assume good intent, default to async, help each other out. 🤝
